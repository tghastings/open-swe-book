The craft behind
the code.

Software engineering is more than software development; it is the discipline of turning ideas into reliable and maintainable systems through informed judgment, effective collaboration, and careful attention to detail.

Software Engineering: Standing on the Shoulders of Giants offers a practical introduction to contemporary software development principles and practices. The text examines the full software development lifecycle, with an emphasis on maintainable, deployable code, compelling use cases, and the growing importance of AI-assisted development.

Intended for students, instructors, and self‑learners, this book connects foundational concepts to authentic engineering tradeoffs. It centers on real, project‑based work — planning, building, evaluating, and refining software the way a team does — presenting the discipline as a hands‑on craft you learn by doing.

About the Author

Thomas Hastings brings over twenty years of experience designing and developing software applications. He teaches software engineering at the University of Colorado Colorado Springs and, as a reservist, at the United States Air Force Academy, and works as a software engineer at Amazon Web Services. He holds a Ph.D. in Engineering and an M.Eng. in Software Engineering from UCCS, and his research focuses on open‑source software security and software supply chains.
